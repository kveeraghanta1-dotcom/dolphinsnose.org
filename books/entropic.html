About this book

Black–Scholes prices an option as if returns were lognormal, volatility constant and trading continuous. None of this is true, and the volatility smile is the proof. The KV Entropic Option Pricing model keeps the one principle no model can do without, the absence of arbitrage, and replaces everything else with data. Its pricing measure is the one closest in relative entropy to the filtered historical law of returns that makes the forward fair and prices downside and upside variance separately.

This book proves the KV Entropic Option Pricing Theorem, derives the call and the put formulas separately, shows that Black–Scholes and Bachelier are exact special cases, and applies the model to equity, commodity, futures, fixed-income, credit, foreign-exchange, digital, variance and multi-asset derivatives. For every product it asks the practical question: does entropic pricing beat Black–Scholes, and when does it not?
