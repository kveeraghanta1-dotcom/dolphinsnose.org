About this book

An AI model earns its place in operational risk only if it makes a key risk indicator more informative about future losses than the indicator it replaces. This book turns that principle into a complete, mathematically grounded toolkit.

Organized around the seven Basel event types, from internal and external fraud to system failures and execution errors, it pairs each original model with a KRI set, the theorems that justify it, worked numerical examples and the controls that make it defensible to a regulator. Twenty-nine KV-enhanced models improve on the baselines, culminating in KV_Risk_Copula, a mixed-family vine copula that keeps both tails when losses are aggregated for capital. More than thirty real loss events, from Barings to Silicon Valley Bank and CrowdStrike, show which indicators bore on what happened.
