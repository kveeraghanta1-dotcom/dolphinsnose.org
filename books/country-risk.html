About this book

Russia defaulted on its ruble debt in 1998 and, with money in hand, on its dollar bonds in 2022. Iran’s rial has lost most of its value in a sequence of sanctions shocks, from 2012 to the war of 2026. This book turns those histories into a theory of country risk — sovereign default, currency collapse, convertibility, sanctions and contagion — with every model stated precisely and every theorem proved.

It then shows asset managers, investors and hedge funds how to hedge country risk, how to size and time positions in it, and when the strategies and the financial products built on it — CDS, credit-linked notes, NDFs, currency options, political risk insurance, trade finance — are profitable. New in the third edition, Part VII covers copulas, vines, stress scenarios and U.S./EU regulation.
