About this book

Fraud, cyber attacks, mis-selling, system outages, processing errors, rogue traders, failed vendors and floods: operational losses are rare, heavy-tailed and dependent, and they cost banks more each decade. This book treats them with the full toolkit of probability, statistics and operations research. Every classical model, from Panjer’s recursion and the loss distribution approach to extreme value theory, vine copulas, Bayesian credibility, Hawkes processes, inspection games and the Basel III standardized measurement approach, is stated, proved and credited to its authors.

Each classical model is then extended. The KV_ models relax restrictive assumptions or add the constraints that practice demands, always containing the classical model as a special case, and every one ends in a number a risk manager can act on: a capital figure, a control budget, a key risk indicator threshold or a decision. Throughout, the methods are applied to Waltair Bank, a hypothetical bank with a complete synthetic loss history.
