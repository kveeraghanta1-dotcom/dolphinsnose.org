About this book

Black–Scholes assumes lognormal prices, constant volatility, continuous replication and a complete market. Markets have fat tails, volatility that clusters and rises after losses, and joint crashes. The Kris Veeraghanta Option Pricing Theory keeps the structure of Black–Scholes — prices are discounted expectations under a measure in which the forward is fair — and replaces every assumption.

The theory unites the KV entropic measure, which prices downside and upside variance separately relative to a data-driven reference law, with the KV_Risk_Copula, a vine of Clayton, Gumbel, Clayton–Gumbel, Frank, Gaussian and Student-t edges. Its central result, the Kris Veeraghanta Option Pricing Theorem, proves existence, uniqueness and absence of arbitrage, reproduces every single-asset smile, introduces a new dependence premium, and nests Black–Scholes, Black-76, Garman–Kohlhagen, Bachelier, Margrabe and the Gaussian copula as special cases.
