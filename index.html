About the author

Kris Veeraghanta: three decades across every line of defense

Kris has spent his career where risk models meet regulation: building and validating quantitative models, running enterprise and model risk programs, and leading the technology that puts them into production. His work spans Value-at-Risk and Expected Shortfall, CCAR and Dodd-Frank stress testing, Basel III capital, SR 11-7 model risk governance, and FR Y-14 and FFIEC 009 regulatory reporting.

He has held roles at Bank of America, Wells Fargo, the Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland, JPMorgan, Merrill Lynch and KPMG. He served as the first Chief Risk Officer, and later COO/CTO, of a hedge fund advisory platform, and most recently as Vice President of Credit Risk Technology at Bank of America. Today he runs an independent consulting practice that brings AI to risk management.

Ph.D. (ABD), Operations Research (Financial Engineering), Ohio State
